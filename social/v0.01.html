superGroup: pods meeting pods

Data dividends

The rules this toy keeps

Invitation only. There is no search for people and no way to add anyone. A code goes out; the other side accepts or doesn't.

Two-way consent. Each person sets how far each layer of them may pass. The pod sees a layer only as far as everyone in it allows: the shared line is the lowest line.

Nothing is aimed at anyone. No profiles of outsiders, no targeting, no ranking of people. Pods, Passforge, superGroups and dividends all run on what members chose to open.

Same passages. A link between two people reads like an atlas link: yes, no, between, history, future, next. The pass cycle ends when the step taken becomes the next yes.
