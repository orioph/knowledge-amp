Your Intelligence Artifact, layer by layer

Every layer has a shade of anonymity. Anonymity is the base; you step out of it only where you choose, and you can sell a layer (disanonymization, chamber 5).

Already in the world (pieces of chamber 1 you can use today)
  • iCloud Private Relay (iCloud+): Safari traffic goes through two relays, so no single company sees both who you are and where you go. The closest real "no one" face.
  • Hide My Email (iCloud+): a different random address for each site.
  • Tor: three-hop onion routing. Stronger anonymity, but slower.
  • Oblivious HTTP (an IETF standard): a relay that strips who sent a request before it reaches the server.

These hide where you are. None of them does what KA's barrier adds: keep everything learned for you, in your own artifact, and let you sell layers of it on your terms.
