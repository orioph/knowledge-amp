# Macroscope: evolution log

Every macroscope run reads this file first and adds one dated entry. An entry records the job, what was built, one new synergy, one new perspective, and one proposed next move. Proposals wait for D's yes, and D's verdicts are added when they come. Nothing is removed: the log is the lineage.

Cycle (the Dandelion): explore, collapse to one, express, relay back.

## Evolution inside the toy (runs on every breath, on that device only)

- A word you return to grows its own labelled bubble in the physical froth.
- The same threads ride the dandelion's seeds.
- Each new sentence is joined to an earlier one through the word they share (the query-learning syllogism).
- Typing a planet name, "galaxy" or "everything" takes you to that level.
